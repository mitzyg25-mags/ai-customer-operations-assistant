# AI Customer Operations Assistant

## Overview

This is a small personal AI learning project exploring how artificial intelligence can help organize customer operations requests and identify the next operational action.

The project was inspired by my previous experience in customer service and logistics, where customer requests often required checking information, coordinating with internal teams, and following up to make sure an issue was resolved.

## The Problem

Customer requests can arrive as unstructured messages containing different types of information, such as shipment status, pickup requests, delays, or questions requiring internal follow-up.

Important details can be difficult to identify quickly, especially when requests need to be reviewed and communicated across different teams.

## The Experiment

I created an AI-assisted workflow that analyzes a customer message and extracts useful operational information, including:

* Request type
* Assets or shipment information mentioned
* Priority
* Customer concern
* Action required
* Recommended next step
* Suggested customer response

The goal is not to replace human decision-making, but to explore how AI can reduce repetitive work and help organize information before it reaches an operations team.

## Example

### Customer message

> Hi, we have two railcars ready for pickup. They have been waiting since yesterday and we would like to know when they will be collected.

### AI analysis

**Request type:** Pickup request

**Priority:** High

**Customer concern:** Pickup delay

**Action required:** Contact Operations and confirm pickup status

**Recommended next step:** Verify the current status of the railcars and provide the customer with an updated pickup estimate.

## What I Am Learning

Through this project, I am exploring:

* How to structure prompts for consistent AI outputs
* How AI can transform unstructured information into structured data
* How AI can support repetitive customer operations tasks
* The importance of human review when AI is used in operational workflows
* How process design and automation can work together

## Background

This project is based on my interest in combining customer service, operations, and emerging AI tools.

I am building this as a learning project to better understand practical applications of AI in everyday business processes.
